## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an understated, high-touch digital sanctuary tailored to private wealth principals, family offices, and executive tastemakers. It translates the tactile precision of bespoke horology, archival stationery, and private concierge services into a high-order executive schedule and reminder interface.

The emotional resonance is anchored in composed clarity, permanence, and discretion. The visual character eschews ephemeral consumer trends, loud synthetic gradients, and industrial blue accents in favor of "Quiet Luxury" and modern editorial minimalism:
- **Atmospheric Balance:** Expansive, deliberate negative space reminiscent of fine art catalog layouts.
- **Architectural Framing:** Hairline metallic boundaries, warm tactile surfaces, and disciplined alignments.
- **Controlled Materiality:** Subtle parchment layering, light-diffused frosted surfaces, and muted warm metallics evoke physical luxury without decorative excess.

## Colors
The palette evokes archival craftsmanship through warmth, restraint, and calculated contrast:

- **Canvas & Base Layers:** Canvas roots in warm alabaster (`#FAF7F2`), moving to parchment beige (`#F4EFEB`) for card layers, and ivory tint (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated focal panels.
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:** Primary typography and structural elements utilize deep espresso obsidian (`#1C1917`), softened by medium obsidian (`#292524`) for secondary metadata and muted peat (`#78716C`) for passive labeling.
- **Accents & Precious Metallics:** Brushed champagne gold (`#C5A880`) serves as the signature interactive accent, deepened to antique brass (`#B38F5B`) on hover and active states.
- **Delicate Boundaries:** Surface contours and grid rules use hairline warm borders (`#E7E1D8`), preserving spatial order without visual clutter.
- **Status & Priority Semantics:** Urgent agendas and priority notices use soft lacquer terracotta (`#C86D51`), while settled commitments, completions, and passive states use muted vintage sage (`#6B7F71`).

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs an authoritative, high-contrast transitional serif with a crisp, geometric sans-serif:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Used for dates, diary headers, primary metrics, and curated section entries. Letterforms are set with slight optical tracking adjustments to evoke luxury periodicals and engraved correspondence.
- **Interface & Metrics (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs time stamps, task parameters, system inputs, metadata, and schedules. Maintains legibility at petite scales without sacrificing warmth.
- **Micro-Labels & Classifiers:** Label typography employs uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to mimic stamped hallmark labels and horological inscriptions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined, architectural grid optimized for sustained concentration and uncluttered schedule oversight:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column fluid grid capped at a maximum width of 1440px with generous margins (`3rem`) to frame content like an open portfolio. Tablets transition to an 8-column layout with `2rem` margins, while mobile interfaces use a 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Rhythm & Negative Space:** Vertical flow relies on intentional whitespace intervals. Sections and discrete timeline blocks maintain ample distance via `space-xl` gaps, avoiding compressed task density.
- **Content Modularization:** Schedule clusters are grouped within structural bays separated by subtle dividers, giving priority items room to breathe without adjacent interface competition.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids synthetic dropshadows or heavy digital layers, opting for optical light-fall and tactile paper stratification:

- **Subtle Ambient Shadows:** Surfaces rest above the base canvas with an ultra-diffused, warm shadow tinted with obsidian and parchment: `0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.02)`.
- **Frosted Translucency:** Floating action panels, time-picker popovers, and sticky agenda heads use subtle frosted glass: `rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.82)` paired with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a hairline metallic top border (`#E7E1D8`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Structural cards do not rely on elevation to demonstrate separation; they sit on parchment tint backgrounds (`#F4EFEB`) bounded by a single, crisp 1px solid border (`#E7E1D8`).

## Shapes
The shape philosophy favors tailored precision over consumer bubble forms:

- **Tailored Radii:** Core containers, task cards, and input frames feature soft architectural corners (`0.25rem` / `4px`), maintaining clean silhouettes that honor editorial grids.
- **Secondary Surfaces:** Larger dialogs, modal canvases, and floating agenda drawers scale subtly to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- **Selection Markers:** Indicator badges, calendar dates, and luxury checkboxes use strict geometric circles or calibrated squares, reinforcing instrument precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep espresso obsidian background (`#1C1917`), warm alabaster text (`#FAF7F2`), hairline champagne border (`#C5A880` at 30% opacity). Hover triggers a transition to antique brass highlight (`#B38F5B`) with a soft glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Parchment background (`#F4EFEB`), 1px hairline border (`#E7E1D8`), obsidian text. Hover shifts background to `#FAF7F2` with border shifting to solid brushed gold (`#C5A880`).
- **Text Action:** Clean serif or uppercase micro-sans with an animated brushed-gold underline transition (`#C5A880`).

### Checkboxes & Affirmation Controls
- Replaces standard utilitarian checkboxes with custom tactile circular and square seal controls.
- Unchecked: 1px hairline ring (`#E7E1D8`) against ivory backing.
- Hover: Border shifts to brushed champagne gold (`#C5A880`).
- Checked / Committed: Solid obsidian fill (`#1C1917`) with an engraved-weight checkmark rendered in champagne gold, accompanied by a muted vintage sage line through completed agenda titles.

### Task & Agenda Cards
- Constructed with parchment backgrounds (`#F4EFEB`), hairline warm borders (`#E7E1D8`), and comfortable inner padding (`space-lg`).
- High-priority reminders incorporate a discrete left-hand vertical bar in soft lacquer terracotta (`#C86D51`).
- Card titles utilize `headline-sm` Playfair Display, paired with metadata (time, location, recurrence) rendered in small Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Chips & Priority Markers
- Minimalist pill badges with `0.25rem` radii.
- Base style uses a transparent body with a 1px border (`#E7E1D8`) and muted peat text (`#78716C`).
- Curated priority tags feature tinted translucent fills: Terracotta Tint (`rgba(200, 109, 81, 0.08)`) with Terracotta text (`#C86D51`) for urgent alerts; Sage Tint (`rgba(107, 127, 113, 0.1)`) with Vintage Sage text (`#6B7F71`) for settled states.

### Input Fields & Pickers
- Flush parchment input panels bounded by hairline warm borders (`#E7E1D8`) with no drop shadow.
- Placeholder text in muted peat (`#78716C`).
- Focused state applies an antique brass hairline highlight (`#B38F5B`) with zero external glow rings, preserving tailored refinement.
- Date and time pickers incorporate dual-column wheels styled after Swiss luxury horology bezels.

### Concierge Reminders Strip (Custom Component)
- A dedicated sticky daily agenda summary anchored at the top of the interface.
- Utilizes frosted alabaster glass (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), an editorial day-and-date title in `headline-md`, and a champagne gold horizontal progress thread tracing the day's timeline.
