## Brand & Style

The brand targets connoisseurs of luxury timekeeping, executive professionals, and aesthetic purists who view their personal devices as precision-crafted instruments rather than commoditized glass slabs. The design language evokes the tactile gravity and hushed poise of fine Swiss horology: serene, unhurried, impeccably calibrated, and profoundly atmospheric.

The design movement combines **Glassmorphism** with **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Micro-details** and classical watchmaking typography:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Multi-tiered frosted parchment glass suspended over deep obsidian abysses.
- **Physical Precision:** Guilloché-inspired hairline dividers, micro-beveled boundaries, metallic brushed accents, and mechanical balance.
- **Micro-tactility:** Push notifications, soundscape widgets, and dynamic complications carry physical weight, simulating satin-finished metal, sapphire crystal lens glares, and luminous dial markers.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical horology, evoking PVD-coated titanium, raw obsidian dials, brushed champagne gold bezels, and luminous tritium patinas.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` - Pure Champagne Gold):** Reserved for primary time indicators, active mechanical complication hands, vital proactive alerts, and authoritative swipe targets.
- **Secondary (`#EED8A1` - Light Warm Gilt):** Deployed for active typography highlights, secondary complication needles, and luminous dial indicators under simulated backlighting.
- **Tertiary (`#8A7968` - Aged Bronze Taupe):** Anchors secondary indicators, inactive indices, ambient complication tracks, and passive status telemetry.
- **Neutral (`#0B0C0E` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational substrate. Deep, light-absorbing obsidian slate providing maximum optical contrast against gold leafing and frosted layers.

### Surface & Frosted Parchment Roles
- **Obsidian Dial Substrate:** `#0B0C0E` solid base with radial micro-vignette.
- **Parchment Blur Glass:** Translucent parchment glaze tinted with warm ivory (`rgba(244, 238, 224, 0.04)` to `rgba(244, 238, 224, 0.08)`), combined with a high-saturation Gaussian backdrop blur.
- **Borders & Chamfers:** Hairline borders tinted with 18k gold sheen (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)` top-edge specular highlight; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` bottom edge).
- **Proactive Amber Accent:** `#E5A93C` specifically tuned for 10-minute predictive alerts and critical horological reminders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-end European editorial lineage with aviation instrument precision:

- **Clock Displays & Prominent Titles (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers classical Roman proportion, extreme vertical stroke contrast, and fine serifs akin to Breguet and Patek Philippe enamel dials. Numbers feature tabular alignments where necessary for complication sync.
- **Push Notification Content & Informational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures flawless legibility at small mobile scale across frosted glass backgrounds without competing with the classical display face.
- **Instrument Readouts, Complications, & Telemetry (`Space Grotesk`):** Fixed-width spirit and mechanical tracking for status bar indicators, soundscape frequency metrics, countdown timers, and horological complication labels. All label tokens must be set in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.18em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Lock screens function within a dynamic, multi-zone contextual viewport engineered specifically for flagship mobile proportions.

### Viewport Zones
1. **Zenith Safe Zone (Top 0–56px):** Luxury OS status bar (cellular signal bars styled as graduated gold pill pips, battery rendered as an engraved watch barrel power reserve meter, dynamic timecode).
2. **Horological Centerpiece (56px–240px):** Dynamic clock face, day/date astronomical complication, and ambient atmospheric telemetry.
3. **Notification & Proactive Stack (Mid-to-Lower Tier):** Expandable frosted glass cards spanning the safe margin. Cards stack chronologically with a 6px peek-under reveal and dynamic physics.
4. **Action & Quick Access Anchor (Bottom 96px):** Soundscape ambient triggers, biometrics status indicator, and dynamic swipe affordance.

### Grid & Padding Architecture
- Mobile canvases adhere to safe margins of `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`), keeping all interactions safe from hardware curved edges.
- Vertical rhythm strictly adheres to multiples of `0.25rem` (`4px`), ensuring flawless alignment of complication ticks, notification dividers, and progress rules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via multi-planar optical refraction rather than standard drop shadows. The lock screen presents three distinct atmospheric strata:

1. **Strata 0 (Dial Substrate):** The foundational surface in `#0B0C0E` featuring a fine circular brushed guilloché pattern layered beneath a subtle 12% black-to-transparent radial falloff.
2. **Strata 1 (Horological Dial & Complication Lenses):** Suspended dial rings, date windows, and mechanical meters. Rendered with inset shadows (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`, `inset 0 -1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`) replicating diamond-cut beveled apertures.
3. **Strata 2 (Parchment Frosted Glass Notifications & Cards):**
   - **Backdrop Filter:** `blur(24px) saturate(180%) brightness(1.1)`.
   - **Base Fill:** `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(244, 238, 224, 0.08) 0%, rgba(11, 12, 14, 0.65) 100%)`.
   - **Specular Edge:** Top and left borders feature `1px solid rgba(238, 216, 161, 0.28)`; bottom and right borders fade into `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
   - **Shadow:** `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
4. **Strata 3 (Proactive 10-Minute Alert Overlays):** Dynamic golden halo bloom using dual-layer gold ambient projection: `0 0 40px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22), 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The geometry balances the precision of machined watch cases with the tactile ergonomics of modern handhelds.

- Standard UI cards (notifications, proactive alert banners) use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`), echoing the soft curvature of tonneau-shaped cases.
- Complication badges, soundscape triggers, and swipe handles employ full pills (`rounded-full`), reflecting circular pushers, crowns, and watch crystals.
- Subtle inner structural cutouts (meter bars, countdown progress tracks) adopt `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to maintain sharp mechanical discipline.

## Components

### 1. Luxury Status Bar
- **Dimensions:** 44px–52px height; full horizontal spread with `margin-mobile` padding.
- **Battery Indicator:** Replaced with an engraved horizontal power reserve complication. Gold tick marks (`0`, `24h`, `48h`, `72h`) with a hairline champagne fluid level.
- **Cellular & Wi-Fi:** Five graduated hairline vertical bars in 18k Champagne (`#D4AF37`), diminishing in opacity for unlinked nodes.
- **Center Telemetry:** Horological standard location name (e.g., `GENÈVE • GMT+1`) in `label-sm`.

### 2. Horological Clock Displays
- **Type Style:** `Bodoni Moda` in `clock-display-mobile` with tabular figures.
- **Dual Complication Pods:** Left and right of the time display:
  - *Left Pod:* Moonphase micro-aperture with realistic parchment-textured lunar disc and starlight pips.
  - *Right Pod:* Barometric / Chronometer precision status in `label-sm`.
- **Sub-dial Seconds:** A sweeping circular hairline dial with ticking champagne gold second hand and micro-dashes every 5 seconds.

### 3. Frosted Parchment Push Notifications
- **Surface:** Frosted parchment glass plate (`blur(24px)`), 1px gold chamfer edge, padding `space-md` (`0.875rem`).
- **Header:** App / Brand monogram in a circular brushed gold seal (`24x24px`), sender title in `headline-sm` (`Plus Jakarta Sans 13px SemiBold`), timestamp formatted as precise horological elapsed time (`+04:12`) in `label-sm`.
- **Body:** `body-md` in `#EED8A1` with 85% opacity for optimal legibility over dial hands.
- **Dismiss / Action Triggers:** Subtle horizontal drag rail with resistance haptic indicators.

### 4. 10-Minute Proactive Alert System
- **Visual Staging:** A prominent frosted pill card framed by a pulsating, slow-breathing champagne gold border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`).
- **Mechanical Gauge:** Features a radial or linear 10-minute retrograde countdown bar that depletes like a mechanical chronograph minute counter.
- **Urgency Indicator:** Luminous amber-gold pip (`#E5A93C`) accompanied by an understated serif headline: `"SYNCHRONIZING IN T-10:00"`.

### 5. Soundscape Triggers & Acoustic Complications
- **Widget Anatomy:** Compact pill card (`space-sm` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal) positioned directly above the swipe handle.
- **Visualizer:** Micro-frequency oscillograph or acoustic waveform composed of 16 hairline vertical bars modulated in real-time in champagne gold.
- **Controls:** Play/Pause glyphs stylized as guilloché pushers with haptic feedback rings. Preset tags (`"CATHEDRAL BELLS"`, `"ALPINE WIND"`, `"TOURBILLON CHIME"`) in `label-sm`.

### 6. Dynamic Swipe Action Bar
- **Track:** 140px width by 4px height recessed hairline canal in `rgba(138, 121, 104, 0.3)`.
- **Handle:** Solid brushed champagne gold bar (`48px` width, `5px` height, `rounded-full`) accompanied by an upward chevron chevron-pulse animation and a faint `label-sm` label: `"SLIDE TO UNLOCK MECHANISM"`.
- **Haptic Ramp:** Progressive haptic resistance replicating a winding mainspring during user swipe.
