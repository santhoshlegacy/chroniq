## Brand & Style

This design system expresses time management not as an urgent backlog of tasks, but as a deliberate architectural composition. The interface draws inspiration from high-end horology, archival stationery, and contemporary stone architecture—specifically raw travertine, warm limestone, and refined metal inlays. 

The aesthetic marries **Quiet Luxury Minimalist** layout discipline with **Tactile Editorial** materiality. Generous open fields, deliberate spatial cadences, hairline metallic dividers, and warm natural undertones replace clinical productivity conventions. The product evokes focus, composure, and curatorial authority: managing an executive schedule or private project feels akin to arranging a master architectural folio.

## Colors

The palette establishes warmth and material richness without synthetic saturation. High-contrast legibility is maintained while avoiding clinical stark white and pure digital black.

### Surface Foundation
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF9F5` / Alabaster Silk):** The foundational backdrop, soft and luminous with an organic mineral cast.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F5F2EB` / Ecru Travertine):** Elevated panels, calendar day cells, and card surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#EFECE3` / Limestone Inset):** Nested modules, input fields, and recessed tracking beds.

### Ink & Typography
- **Primary Ink (`#181615` / Deep Espresso):** Supreme contrast for headings, hero metrics, and prominent text without harsh coldness.
- **Secondary Ink (`#2D2926` / Sable Brown):** Body copy, agenda metadata, and editorial descriptions.
- **Tertiary Ink (`#7A746E` / Warm Stone Grey):** Timestamps, secondary labels, unselected day names, and subtle metrics.

### Metal Accents & Finishes
- **Champagne Gold (`#C2A26F`):** Primary highlights, active scheduling bars, focus rings, and milestone flourishes.
- **Polished Bronze (`#8C7654`):** Secondary controls, structural badges, and borders on active modules.

### Status Indicators (Muted Mineral Accents)
- **Success / Completed (`#4A6B56` / Imperial Jade):** Subdued, botanical green for accomplished milestones.
- **Urgent / Milestone (`#A65D46` / Baked Terracotta):** Earthy crimson-terracotta denoting critical timeline thresholds.
- **In-Progress / Focus (`#C2A26F` / Champagne Gold):** Warm illumination for active state.

## Typography

Typography establishes tension between literary heritage and technical precision:
- **Playfair Display:** Reserved for dates, editorial section openings, major project titles, and empty-state reflections. It provides high-fashion authority and timeless prestige.
- **Inter:** Serves as the razor-sharp grotesque engine for schedules, micro-copy, time durations, tables, and task labels. All numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) leverage Inter's crisp geometries to feel like precision-calibrated timekeeping instruments.
- **Casing & Spacing:** Labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) default to uppercase with extended tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em - 0.08em`) to mimic etched hallmarks on metal and stone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in an architectural 12-column grid featuring intentional asymmetry, spacious margins, and breathable visual buffers.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Outer canvas margin of `3rem` (`48px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Primary executive views separate timeline chronologies (7 columns) from agenda curation & project dossiers (5 columns).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** `2rem` (`32px`) margins with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Split screens collapse into layered sliding panels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters. Focus narrows to a single horizon timeline, with tasks stacked vertically.
- **Rhythm Philosophy:** Whitespace is treated as active material. Large empty spans between schedule milestones signify cognitive decompression rather than unutilized real estate.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through tactile materiality and nuanced ambient warmth, rather than harsh artificial shadows.

1. **Surface Stratification:** Depth relies primarily on tonal shifts across natural stone values (`#FBF9F5` base to `#F5F2EB` surface to `#EFECE3` recessed wells).
2. **Hairline Precision Inlays:** Elevated containers use crisp 1px borders tinted in warm bronze-espresso at ultra-low opacity (`rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.08)`), accented with subtle champagne gold highlights on focused states (`rgba(194, 162, 111, 0.35)`).
3. **Warm Ambient Umbra:** Floating panels (dropdown menus, active scheduling drawers, modal sheets) employ soft, diffused drop shadows with a warm sable cast:
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.03)`
4. **Frosted Silk Glassmorphism:** Translucent toast alerts, time ticker bars, and floating action headers utilize semi-transparent backing with heavy blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.82)`), evoking sandblasted architectural glass.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural rectilinear geometry tempered with restrained softness (Level 1 - Soft).

- **Standard Elements (0.25rem / 4px):** Checkbox boxes, micro tags, status dots, and task selector chips maintain structured, geometric profiles.
- **Medium Panels & Inputs (0.5rem / 8px):** Schedule blocks, form fields, and dropdown drawers feature softly softened edges that retain structural posture.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (0.75rem / 12px):** Top-level dialogs and drawer containers adopt gentle curvature to signal detachment from the background canvas.
- **Strict Avoidance:** Large bubble radiuses and circular pill buttons are explicitly prohibited to preserve the formal architectural tone.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Espresso (`#181615`) fill with Alabaster (`#FBF9F5`) typography in Inter 13px Semi-bold. Hover initiates a gentle lift and a subtle champagne edge tint (`#C2A26F`).
- **Secondary (Architectural Frame):** Transparent background with a 1px border of Sable Brown at 15% opacity (`rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.15)`), shifting to brushed Champagne Gold on hover.
- **Ghost / Minimalist:** Unframed sable text paired with an understated arrow or indicator, showing a 1px baseline underline on hover.

### Chips & Meta Indicators
- **Category Chips:** Rectilinear, 4px radius, ecru background (`#EFECE3`), styled in 11px uppercase Inter with `letter-spacing: 0.08em`.
- **Status Pills:** Travertine neutral base accented by a 6px solid gemstone pip (Imperial Jade for completion, Baked Terracotta for critical deadlines, Champagne Gold for active focus).

### Checkboxes & Task Completion
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px squares with 3px border radius and a 1px hairline border (`#8C7654` at 40%).
- **Interaction:** Checking triggers a subtle 280ms spring animation; the fill transitions to Warm Sable with a delicate Champagne checkmark, and task copy shifts smoothly to muted grey with a refined horizontal strike.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Styling:** Subtle limestone fill (`#EFECE3`) with no bottom or side shadow. Features an inset hairline border that transitions to brushed champagne gold (`#C2A26F`) on focus.
- **Typography:** Labels rest above inputs in 11px uppercase Inter; input text renders in 15px high-contrast espresso.

### Cards & Dossiers
- **Project & Schedule Cards:** Travertine background (`#F5F2EB`) surrounded by a 1px hairline edge. Zero default drop shadow; hover triggers an organic 1px vertical translation and an ultra-diffused warm sable ambient glow.
- **Internal Padding:** Generous padding (`space-lg` / `1.75rem`) ensures contents never crowd card borders.

### Signature Time Block Indicator (Chroniq Horizon)
- Continuous vertical timeline axis represented by a razor-thin 1px warm bronze rule (`#C2A26F` at 25%).
- Current time marker displays as a clean geometric dial: a 7px polished bronze bead holding an ultra-sharp horizontal hairline across the day's schedule grid.
